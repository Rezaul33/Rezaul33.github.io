Research & Publications

Selected research work and publications

Enhancing Game Usability: A Framework for Small-to-Medium-Sized Game Development Businesses

Scitepress • Jan 2023

This study addresses game usability for small studios in Bangladesh, proposing twelve heuristics based on Nielsen’s principles to help developers identify and prevent common usability issues during game development.

Renewable Energy Optimization: The Role of Solar Tracking Systems

IEEE • Aug 2023

To reduce reliance on fossil fuels and protect the environment, renewable energy is essential. This paper proposes a low-power single-axis solar tracking system that increases solar panel efficiency by improving energy output under varying conditions compared to fixed panels.

Green Computing Adoption: Understanding the Role of Individual, Social, and Organizational Factors

Scitepress • Jan 2024

This study examines factors affecting Bangladeshi IT professionals’ attitudes toward Green IT, showing how awareness, knowledge, and perceptions influence green computing practices, and highlights the adoption of sustainable IT in software firms.

Project Governance to Improve the Performance of Software Projects by Mitigating the Software Risk Factors: The Moderating Role of Project Leadership

Elsevier • Jan 2024

This study finds that project governance improves Bangladeshi software project performance by reducing risk factors, while effective project leadership strengthens this effect, highlighting the value of governance systems in managing software project risks.

Overcoming Obstacles in Model-Driven Engineering: Lessons from the Software Industry

Scitepress • May 2024

This study surveys Bangladesh’s software industry on Model-Driven Engineering, finding 75% use system modeling, with 56% seeing efficiency gains, and suggests research, training, documentation, and scalability measures to address challenges and improve adoption.
